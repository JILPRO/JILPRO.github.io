# Privacy Policy for Carte Mentale Pionnière

**Effective Date:** September 24, 2026

## Introduction
This Privacy Policy applies to the **Carte Mentale Pionnière** mobile application (Package: `com.jilpro.cartementalepionniere`), developed and maintained by **JILPRO** (Developer: Karim Aghbal). 

Our application is designed as an educational tool for teachers and educators in Moroccan "Pionnier Schools" (مدارس الريادة) to generate pedagogical Mind Maps (Cartes Mentales) and Daily Lesson Journals (Cahier Journal) from presentation files. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

---

## Data Collection and Usage

### 1. Personal Data
**Carte Mentale Pionnière does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal identification information.** 
- All data entered into the app (lesson titles, session timings, teacher stamps, daily notes, and preferences) is saved **strictly locally** on your device using Android's local Room database and SharedPreferences.
- We do not operate external databases, user registration systems, or remote user tracking servers for personal data.

### 2. Local File & Storage Access
- The app requests access to local storage only to import user-selected PPTX lesson files and to export/save generated PDF documents and images locally on your device.
- Imported lesson content is processed entirely locally on the device or via prompt text copied directly by the user to external AI assistants (e.g., Gemini or Claude) at the user's sole discretion.

## Third-Party Services & Advertisements

### Google AdMob & UMP SDK
Our application integrates **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive advertisements and support app development.

- **COPPA & Family Policy Compliance:** Since our application is intended for educators and the educational ecosystem, we enforce strict ad filtering:
  - **Child-Directed Treatment:** `TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE`
  - **Maximum Content Rating:** `MAX_AD_CONTENT_RATING_G` (General Audiences suitable for all users)
- **Non-Personalized Ads:** We do not track users across other apps or websites for targeted advertising.
- **User Messaging Platform (UMP):** The app uses Google's UMP SDK to handle privacy consent requests in compliance with GDPR and Google Play policies.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please refer to:
[Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Our app complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All ad content served is strictly filtered for general audience suitability.
